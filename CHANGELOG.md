# Rocket Mount 0.32.0

- The horseshoe button on the world map now opens a menu: hide every marker, show only the mounts
  you are missing (the default), or the collected ones too. A place where you already have every
  mount gets a gold ring, and its tooltip says "Collected" next to each mount you have.
- Fixed: after opening the support or report dialog, typing DELETE to destroy an item could stop
  working.
- The tooltips of the support and report links follow the game's style, and secondary text is
  easier to read.
