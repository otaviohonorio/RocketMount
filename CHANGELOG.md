# Rocket Mount 0.24.0

- **Go to where the mount is, from the list.** Every row whose mount has a known place now has
  the game's map pin: one click marks the place and opens the world map on it. It works for
  any kind of mount -- the vendor, the quest giver, the rare, the chest -- and for raid and
  dungeon mounts, where the place is the way in to the instance.
- **Markers stay near their place.** On a crowded map (a continent, a capital) the markers
  that would overlap were opened into one huge ring, far from where each thing is. Each one
  now steps aside only as much as it takes and never far from its place; on a continent they
  sit closer still, since the zone's map is where each is seen.
- **Marker size.** Two new options set how large the markers are, on the world map and on the
  minimap, from 80% to 150%.
- The mount's card has two buttons for the same place, **Set map pin** and **Open the map**,
  now also for raid and dungeon mounts.
