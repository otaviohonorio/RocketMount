# Rocket Mount 0.31.0

- With TomTom installed, the rare alert also puts the rare's place in it, with its arrow. The pin
  link in the chat stays, and "Send to TomTom" in the options turns it off.
- Every open-world rare was checked again for routes. Rares that appear at several places no longer
  have a line drawn between those places, rares that patrol got their route (some had none, one was
  a zig-zag through the middle of the zone), and a few places were corrected.
- The Time-Lost Proto-Drake shows its four spawn points, each with its own flight.
- When the routes of two rares meet on a map, they are drawn in different colours, and the marker's
  tooltip says which line is its own.
- Beledar's Spawn now says when its event ends, not only when it starts, in your computer's time.
