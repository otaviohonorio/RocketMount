# Rocket Mount 0.37.0

- New look for the mount list: each mount is a card, and a click opens its details right under it, with the buttons at the end.
- The selected mount's 3D model now shows in a panel docked beside the window.
- The options moved into the addon's own window, in the Settings tab. Options > AddOns has a button that opens it.
- Fixed a "script ran too long" error that could show right after logging in.
- New Rocket family logo in the AddOns list, the minimap button and the window. The Rocket addons now sit together in the game's AddOns list.
- The window footer keeps only **Report a problem**.
- Missing translations filled in, in every language of the game.
