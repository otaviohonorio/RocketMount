# Rocket Mount 0.36.0

- **Now in 11 languages**: Spanish (Spain and Mexico), Italian, German, French, Russian, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), besides English and Brazilian Portuguese. It follows your game's language. Game terms are the game's own words in each language. The new translations were not reviewed by native speakers yet: corrections are welcome in the comments.
- Event alerts now say the map and the place; clicking one points the game's arrow there (and TomTom's, when you have it). The on-screen alert is optional, and alerts never show in combat or inside an instance.
- Prices in a currency or an item show its icon, as gold already did.
- In the list, the line that says what is missing wraps to two lines instead of being cut; the whole sentence is in the tooltip.
- Brazilian Portuguese texts reviewed.
